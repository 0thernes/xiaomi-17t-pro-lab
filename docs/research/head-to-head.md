## 4. Benchmarks (named labs; no cherry-picking)

### 4.1 Geekbench 6 (do not mix with GB7)

PhoneArena’s **17T Pro review** ran Geekbench 6 in one session against the S26 Ultra (and other Androids):

| Geekbench 6 | 17T Pro | S26 Ultra | Source |
| :--- | ---: | ---: | :--- |
| Single | **2903** | **3753** | [PhoneArena 17T Pro review](https://www.phonearena.com/reviews/xiaomi-17t-pro-review_id7943) |
| Multi | **9145** | **11259** | same |

GSMArena’s 17T Pro figures (different lab/session) are **higher** for the Xiaomi: **3158 / 9766** ([GSMArena comparison / review coverage](https://www.gsmarena.com/xiaomi_17t_pro-review-2965p4.php)). That is still **below** PhoneArena’s S26 Ultra GB6 multi of 11259, but it shows run-to-run / lab spread. Both numbers are reported; neither is discarded.

A **same-lab Geekbench 6** result for the **iPhone 18 Pro Max** alongside those two was **not clearly isolated** in the sources used here. PhoneArena’s aggregate “benchmarks” table lists 18 Pro Max scores that appear under a mixed **v7/v6** heading and **must not** be treated as GB6. Aggregator pages (e.g. pcspecs.io citing 4722/12626) are **not** used as primary results. **iPhone GB6: not ranked here without a same-version citation.**

**Win / loss (GB6, PhoneArena lab):** S26 Ultra **beats** 17T Pro by a wide margin (~29% single, ~23% multi). PhoneArena itself notes daily use still felt “flawless” on the Xiaomi, but “pure CPU performance was noticeably behind.” That loss is real.

### 4.2 3DMark — PhoneArena, same methodology

From the 17T Pro review (17T vs S26 Ultra) and PhoneArena’s later **iPhone 18 Pro Max vs S26 Ultra** article (same Steel Nomad Light stress protocol):

| Test | 17T Pro | S26 Ultra | 18 Pro Max | Source |
| :--- | ---: | ---: | ---: | :--- |
| Wild Life Extreme **High** | **6363** | **7801** | — (not in the 17T review table) | [PA 17T review](https://www.phonearena.com/reviews/xiaomi-17t-pro-review_id7943) |
| Wild Life Extreme **Low** (stress) | **3377** | **3741** | — | same |
| Steel Nomad Light **High** | **2476** | **2761** | **3850** | 17T review; [PA iPhone vs Ultra](https://www.phonearena.com/news/I-tested-the-iPhone-18-Pro-Maxs-A20-Pro-performance-against-the-Galaxy-S26-Ultra_id183453) |
| Steel Nomad Light **Low** (stress) | **1871** | **1498** | **2443** | same two articles |

Stability ratios (Low/High, computed from the table, not a vendor claim):

- 17T Pro SNL: 1871/2476 ≈ **76%**
- S26 Ultra SNL: 1498/2761 ≈ **54%**
- 18 Pro Max SNL: 2443/3850 ≈ **63%**

**Wins / losses (GPU benches):**

- **Peak GPU:** iPhone 18 Pro Max **wins** Steel Nomad Light High (3850), then S26 Ultra (2761), then 17T Pro (2476). On Wild Life Extreme High, S26 Ultra (7801) **beats** 17T Pro (6363). iPhone WLE High in *that same 17T review table* is absent.
- **Sustained Steel Nomad Light Low:** iPhone still **wins** (2443). The 17T Pro’s Low score (**1871**) **beats the S26 Ultra (1498)** — PhoneArena called this a surprise and credited cooling. That is a genuine Xiaomi **win versus the Ultra**, and a **loss versus the iPhone**.
- Wild Life Extreme Low: S26 Ultra 3741 slightly ahead of 17T Pro 3377 — Xiaomi **does not** win this older-path stress test.

No cherry-pick: Xiaomi trails both on peak raster/path-traced style scores, trails the iPhone on sustained Nomad, but holds up better than the Ultra in that particular Nomad stress low score.

**Wins / losses (hardware only):**

- **Main sensor resolution / aperture range:** Samsung **wins** 200 MP and f/1.4; Apple **wins** variable-aperture control (unique among the three). Xiaomi’s f/1.67 50 MP is competitive but **not** the spec-sheet leader.
- **Telephoto:** Samsung **wins** flexibility (dedicated 3× *and* 5×). Xiaomi **wins a 50 MP 5×** vs Samsung’s 50 MP 5× (tie on that class) and vs Apple’s 4× 48 MP (Xiaomi has longer native 115 mm / 5×). Apple **wins 8× optical-quality** tetraprism reach (200 mm).
- **Ultra-wide resolution:** Apple 48 MP and Samsung 50 MP **beat** Xiaomi’s 12 MP UW — a clear **loss** for the 17T Pro.
- **Front camera resolution:** 17T Pro **32 MP** beats Apple **18 MP** Center Stage (ƒ/1.9) and Samsung **12 MP**. Aperture: Apple’s ƒ/1.9 is faster than both Androids’ f/2.2. This is hardware only, not selfie-quality.
- **8K video:** Xiaomi and Samsung list 8K30; Apple’s cited table does not. **Win** for the two Androids on that checkbox; Apple **wins** ProRes/Log2/Dolby Vision creator formats.
- This is **not** a photo-quality ranking (no DXOMARK/studio shoot was used).
