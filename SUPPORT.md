# Support

## Getting Help

- **Usage & reproducibility questions:** Start with [README.md](README.md), [REPLICATION.md](REPLICATION.md), and [docs/faq.md](docs/faq.md).
- **Hardware provenance disputes or new primary teardowns:** Open an issue using the **Bug Report** or **Falsification / Error** form in `.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/` with a primary URL, measurement date, and exact `data/specs.json` key.
- **Formal verification or numerical discrepancies:** Report the exact failing command (`just test`, `just oracle`, or `just lean`), OS/toolchain version, and observed vs. expected output.
- **Security disclosures:** Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) or email `0_0@0thernes.art`.

## Maintenance Scope

This project is maintained by an independent researcher on a best-effort basis (`Status: active`). Pull requests are not required; issue reports with reproducible evidence are welcome and addressed directly on `main`.
